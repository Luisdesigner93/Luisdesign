Services

Three roles you would normally hire separately.

Product Owner

I represent your business in front of the tech team. I turn what the client needs into clear user stories, order the backlog and check that every delivery meets expectations.

Includes: business understanding, user stories, roadmap and prioritized backlog, Scrum ceremonies, QA of every story, expectation management and progress reports. I also coordinate specialized cells inside your client’s development team.

CasesLegisnova · Call auditor · Multi-agent virtual assistant

Brand Strategy for Products

A product without an identity is harder to explain and harder to sell. I define the purpose, voice and identity of the product or of the company behind it, so the team and the users share the same direction.

Includes: Brand Compass, naming, visual identity, voice and website.

CasesBytequest brand · Yomecontrolo

UX/UI and AI-assisted Prototypes

I design the experience and take it to a working prototype with AI-assisted development, to validate before investing in development. Then I hand it over to your team for production.

Includes: flows, clickable or working prototypes, interface design and handover to the development team.

CasesAuditorWeb · Legisnova website and platform · Yomecontrolo web app

Method

Scrum, with both feet in the business.

From day one we agree on priorities, owners and deliverables, and I check the warning signs every sprint to correct course in time.

  1. Step 01

    Understand the Business

    I meet with your team and your client to understand who the users are, what they need and what is expected from the product.

  2. Step 02

    Define

    I write the user stories, build the roadmap and we prioritize together what gets built first.

  3. Step 03

    Build in Sprints

    The team delivers progress in short cycles; I coordinate, clear blockers and keep everyone informed.

  4. Step 04

    Review

    I QA every user story: it isn’t done until it meets expectations.

  5. Step 05

    Adjust

    At every review we look at what was built, learn and correct course.

About

A designer with a product mindset.

I’m Luis Vicencio, a designer from Universidad de Valparaíso, with studies in Product Design for Innovation at Politecnico di Milano and training as a Product Owner and in Scrum. I started out designing brands and, project by project, added strategy and product leadership.

At Yomecontrolo I learned that a good brand strategy needs product tools to reach production. Today I support both stages: I give the product a direction and take it step by step, deliverable by deliverable, until every delivery meets expectations.

Frequently asked questions.

What does a fractional Product Owner do?

They represent your business in front of the tech team: they define what gets built and in what order, and check that every delivery meets expectations, without you having to hire someone full time.

Do you work with my development team?

Yes. I join your team or your client’s, as I did coordinating an AI cell inside the team of a Bytequest client.

Do you code?

I design and build working prototypes with AI-assisted development to validate ideas quickly. Production development is done by your team.

Do you have experience with AI?

Yes: I led products with multi-agent assistants, automatic call analysis, AI summaries and automations. I also work with spec-driven development: I write clear specifications so your team and AI tools build exactly what was agreed.

Do you sign confidentiality agreements?

Yes. That is why some cases are shown without the client’s name.
