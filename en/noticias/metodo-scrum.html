Scrum, grounded in the business

From the start we agree on priorities, owners and deliverables, and I review warning signs in every sprint to correct course in time.

Author
Luis Vicencio
Category
Method
  1. Understand the business. I meet with your team and your client to understand who the users are, what they need and what is expected from the product.
  2. Define. I write the user stories, build the roadmap, and we prioritize together what gets built first.
  3. Build in sprints. The team delivers progress in short cycles; I coordinate, clear blockers and keep every party informed.
  4. Review. I QA every user story: it is not considered done until it meets expectations.
  5. Adjust. At each review we look at what was built, learn and correct course.

What I learned

I learned to recognize the early warning signs of a project: vague priorities, decisions without a clear owner and expectations not agreed. Today I check them from day one.
