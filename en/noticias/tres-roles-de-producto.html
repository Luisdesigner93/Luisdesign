Three roles you usually hire separately

Product Owner, brand strategy for products, and UX/UI with AI prototypes. All three in one person, with a clear direction.

Product Owner

I represent your business in front of the tech team. I turn what the client needs into clear user stories, order the backlog and check that every delivery meets expectations.

Includes: understanding of the business, user stories, roadmap and prioritized backlog, Scrum ceremonies, QA of each story, expectation management and progress reports.

Brand strategy for products

A product without identity is explained worse and sells worse. I define the purpose, voice and identity of the product or of the company that builds it.

Includes: Brand Compass, naming, visual identity, voice and website.

UX/UI and AI prototypes

I design the experience and take it to a functional prototype with AI-assisted development, to validate before investing in development. Then I hand it over to your team for production.

Includes: flows, clickable or functional prototypes, interface design and handover to the development team.
